🌱 Basic Information

Common Name: Chamomile

Botanical Name: Matricaria chamomilla

Chamomile is a well-known medicinal herb famous for its calming properties and is widely used in herbal teas, skincare, and traditional medicine.

🌿 Medicinal Uses

😌 Relaxation & Stress Relief

😴 Sleep Aid

🦠 Anti-inflammatory

🍵 Digestive Health

💆 Skin Care

🌍 Habitat

Native to Europe and Western Asia, now grown worldwide.

🌾 Cultivation

Sunlight: Full sunlight

Soil: Well-drained soil

Watering: Moderate

Propagation: Seeds

⚠️ Precautions

Safe in moderate use.

May cause allergies in some individuals.

Consult doctor if needed.
