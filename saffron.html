🧬 Overview

Saffron (Crocus sativus) is one of the most valuable and rare medicinal plants in the world. It is known for antioxidant, mood-enhancing, and healing properties.

🌱 Botanical Information

Family: Iridaceae

Common Name: Saffron

Plant Type: Perennial flowering plant

Parts Used: Stigmas (threads)

Habitat: Temperate regions

🌿 Cultivation

  • Well-drained sandy or loamy soil
  • Cool winters and dry summers
  • Full sunlight exposure
  • Propagation through corms

🧪 Key Bioactive Compounds

  • Crocin: Antioxidant
  • Safranal: Mood enhancer
  • Picrocrocin: Bitter compound
  • Flavonoids

💊 Medicinal Benefits

  • Improves mood
  • Supports heart health
  • Anti-inflammatory
  • Enhances memory

🩺 Therapeutic Uses

  • Mental wellness
  • Cardiovascular support
  • Digestive health

⚙️ Mechanism of Action

  • Boosts serotonin
  • Neutralizes free radicals
  • Improves circulation

⚠️ Safety & Precautions

  • Safe in small quantities
  • High doses may cause effects

🧬 Nutritional Value

Contains vitamins A, C, manganese, and antioxidants.

🍽️ Culinary Uses

Used in biryani, desserts, milk, and beverages.

🌱 Propagation Tips

Corms planted in autumn for best flowering.

🌍 Modern Importance

Used in pharmaceuticals and cosmetics.
