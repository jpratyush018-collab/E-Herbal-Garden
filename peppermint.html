🧬 Overview

Peppermint (Mentha × piperita) is a hybrid mint plant known for its refreshing aroma, cooling sensation, and therapeutic properties.

🌱 Botanical & General Information

Family: Lamiaceae

Common Name: Peppermint

Plant Type: Perennial herb

Habitat: Temperate regions

Parts Used: Leaves, stems, oils

🌿 Propagation & Cultivation

Vegetative Propagation: Stem cuttings

Seeds: Less common

🌞 Growth Requirements

  • Moist soil
  • Partial to full sunlight
  • Cool climate
  • Regular watering

🧪 Key Bioactive Compounds

  • Menthol
  • Menthone
  • Flavonoids
  • Tannins

💊 Medicinal Properties

  • Digestive aid
  • Analgesic
  • Antimicrobial
  • Anti-inflammatory

🩺 Therapeutic Applications

  • Digestive comfort
  • Relaxation
  • Pain relief

⚙️ Mechanism of Action

  • Cooling effect
  • Microbial inhibition
  • Antioxidant action

⚠️ Safety

Safe in moderate amounts.
