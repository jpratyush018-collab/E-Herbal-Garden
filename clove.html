🧬 Overview

Clove (Syzygium aromaticum) is a highly valued medicinal and aromatic plant known for its antimicrobial, analgesic, and therapeutic properties.

The dried flower buds are the main part used, containing potent bioactive compounds responsible for its medicinal effects.

🌱 Botanical & General Information

Family: Myrtaceae

Common Name: Clove / Laung

Plant Type: Evergreen tree

Habitat: Tropical regions

Parts Used: Dried flower buds (main), leaves, oil

🌿 Propagation & Cultivation

Seeds: Sown directly in warm, humid soil.

Vegetative Methods: Rarely used.

🌞 Growth Requirements

  • Warm, humid climate
  • Fertile, well-drained soil
  • Partial shade to full sunlight
  • Regular watering

🧪 Key Bioactive Compounds

  • Eugenol
  • Caryophyllene
  • Tannins
  • Flavonoids
  • Essential oils

💊 Medicinal Properties

  • Antimicrobial
  • Analgesic
  • Anti-inflammatory
  • Antioxidant
  • Digestive Support

🩺 Therapeutic Applications

  • Dental Care
  • Digestive Health
  • Infection Control
  • General Wellness

⚙️ Mechanism of Action

  • Disrupts microbial growth
  • Blocks pain signals
  • Reduces inflammation
  • Provides antioxidant protection

⚠️ Safety & Precautions

  • Safe in moderate amounts
  • Excess may cause irritation
