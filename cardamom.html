🧬 Overview

Cardamom (Elettaria cardamomum) is a highly valued medicinal and aromatic plant known for its digestive, antimicrobial, and therapeutic properties.

The seeds enclosed in pods are the primary part used.

🌱 Botanical & General Information

Common Name: Cardamom

Botanical Name: Elettaria cardamomum

Family: Zingiberaceae

Plant Type: Perennial herb

Habitat: Tropical regions

Parts Used: Seeds (main), pods

Cardamom produces small green pods containing aromatic seeds.

🌿 Propagation & Cultivation

  • Rhizome division
  • Seeds
  • Warm humid climate
  • Partial shade

🧪 Key Bioactive Compounds

  • Cineole
  • Terpinyl acetate
  • Limonene
  • Flavonoids

💊 Medicinal Properties

  • Digestive support
  • Antimicrobial
  • Respiratory support
  • Antioxidant

🩺 Therapeutic Applications

  • Digestive health
  • Respiratory relief
  • Oral health

⚙️ Mechanism of Action

  • Improves digestion
  • Prevents infections
  • Protects cells

⚠️ Precautions

  • Safe in moderate use
  • Excess may cause side effects
