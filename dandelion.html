🌱 Basic Information

Common Name: Dandelion

Botanical Name: Taraxacum officinale

Dandelion is a common flowering plant often considered a weed, but it is highly valued in herbal medicine.

🌿 Medicinal Uses

🩺 Liver Health

🍵 Digestive Aid

💧 Diuretic

🛡️ Antioxidant

🩸 Blood Purifier

🌍 Habitat

Grows worldwide in temperate regions.

🌾 Cultivation

Sunlight: Full sun

Soil: Fertile soil

Watering: Moderate

Propagation: Seeds

⚠️ Precautions

Safe in moderate amounts.

May cause allergies in some people.

Excess may cause discomfort.
