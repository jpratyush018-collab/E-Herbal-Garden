🧬 Overview

Cinnamon is a widely used medicinal and aromatic plant, known for its antimicrobial, metabolic, and antioxidant benefits.

🌱 Botanical Information

Common Name: Cinnamon

Botanical Name: Cinnamomum verum / Cinnamomum cassia

Family: Lauraceae

Plant Type: Evergreen tree

Parts Used: Inner bark (main), leaves, essential oil

Habitat: Tropical regions

🌿 Cultivation & Propagation

  • Seeds: Sown under warm, moist conditions
  • Vegetative Methods: Stem cuttings, layering
  • Warm, humid climate
  • Fertile soil
  • Regular watering

🧪 Key Bioactive Compounds

  • Cinnamaldehyde
  • Eugenol
  • Coumarin
  • Polyphenols
  • Flavonoids

💊 Medicinal Benefits

  • Regulates blood sugar
  • Antimicrobial
  • Antioxidant
  • Supports heart health

🩺 Therapeutic Applications

  • Diabetes management
  • Digestive health
  • Immunity support

⚙️ Mechanism of Action

  • Improves insulin sensitivity
  • Prevents microbial growth
  • Reduces inflammation

🧬 Nutritional Value

Rich in minerals and antioxidants.

🍽️ Culinary Uses

Used in desserts, beverages, and spices.

⚠️ Safety & Precautions

  • Safe in moderate use
  • Excess may cause toxicity
