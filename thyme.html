🌿 Thyme (Thymus vulgaris)

The Antiseptic and Healing Herb

Home Plants About Contact

🌱 Basic Information

Common Name: Thyme

Botanical Name: Thymus vulgaris

Family: Lamiaceae

Origin: Mediterranean Region

Plant Type: Perennial herb

Parts Used: Leaves, flowering tops, essential oil

🌿 Medicinal Uses

Natural antiseptic and antimicrobial

Relieves cough, cold, bronchitis, and respiratory infections

Supports digestive health and reduces bloating

Boosts immunity and antioxidant defense

Helps manage inflammation

🌍 Habitat

Thyme thrives in sunny, dry climates with well-drained soil.

🌾 Cultivation

Propagation: Seeds, cuttings, or division

Soil: Well-drained sandy or loamy soil

Sunlight: Full sun

Watering: Moderate

Harvest: Leaves after 4–6 months

⚠️ Precautions

Excessive use of thyme oil may cause irritation.

🧪 Chemical Compounds

Thymol: Antiseptic

Carvacrol: Antibacterial

Flavonoids: Antioxidant

💊 Pharmacological Properties

Antimicrobial, antiseptic, antioxidant, anti-inflammatory

🩺 Therapeutic Applications

Used for cough, cold, digestive issues

⚙️ Mechanism of Action

Thymol and carvacrol disrupt microbes

🧬 Nutritional Value

Rich in vitamins C and A, iron

🍽️ Culinary Uses

Used in soups, sauces, vegetables, meats

🌱 Propagation Tips

Cuttings grow faster than seeds

🌍 Modern Relevance

Used in herbal medicine and aromatherapy
