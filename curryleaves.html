🌱 Basic Information

Common Name: Curry Leaves / Kadi Patta

Botanical Name: Murraya koenigii

Family: Rutaceae

Origin: India

🌿 Medicinal Uses

Curry leaves help in improving digestion and reducing acidity.

They help control blood sugar levels.

Promote hair growth and prevent greying.

🌍 Habitat

Grows in tropical and subtropical climates.

🌾 Cultivation

Grown from seeds or stem cuttings.

⚠️ Precautions

Safe but excess may cause discomfort.

🧪 Chemical Compounds

Carbazole Alkaloids: Antioxidant

Vitamin A & C: Boost immunity

Flavonoids: Anti-inflammatory
